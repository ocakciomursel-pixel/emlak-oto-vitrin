# PortföyTR Multi-Theme Design System Source of Truth

Derived from `VoltAgent/awesome-design-md` (`airbnb`, `ferrari`, `bmw`, `mercury`) + `taste-skill` (`place-carries-it`, `app-screens`) + `frontend-design`.

1. **Tema 1 (`tema-1-vitrin`) — Modern Pazar Yeri & Haritalı Keşif (`Airbnb` Design System)**
   - Canvas `#FFFFFF`, Soft Surface `#F7F7F7`, Ink `#222222`, Hairline `#DDDDDD`.
   - Floating `9999px` pill search bar, `16px` rounded photography cards, interactive split-map with live price pins, tactile spring micro-interactions, 4 color palettes (`Rausch #FF385C`, `Ocean #0066CC`, `Emerald #0F766E`, `Midnight #14161A`).

2. **Tema 2 (`tema-2-prestij`) — Mimari Rezidans & Lüks Oto Showroom (`Ferrari` + `BMW` + `place-carries-it`)**
   - Canvas `#141518`, Elevated `#1E2127`, Sharp `2px–4px` architectural geometry, `Space Grotesk` + `Instrument Serif` editorial contrast.
   - Full-bleed hero showcase with shutter reveal, interactive top-down **SVG Vehicle Kaporta Ekspertiz Blueprint**, mortgage/installment calculator, 4 color palettes (`Rosso #DA291C`, `Bavarian Blue #1C69D4`, `Champagne Gold #C89D54`, `Ivory Showroom #F7F7F5`).

3. **Tema 3 (`tema-3-kurumsal`) — Pro İlan Portalı & Tablo/Karşılaştırma Sistemi (`BMW Corporate` + `Mercury` + `app-screens`)**
   - Canvas `#F6F7F9`, Panel `#FFFFFF`, Tabular monospace numerals (`JetBrains Mono`), high-density left filter tree.
   - Instant switch between **Pro Tablo Satır Görünümü** and **Kompakt Vitrin Görünümü**, **Yan Yana İlan Karşılaştırma Çekmecesi**, slide-over detail sheet, 4 color palettes (`Portal Sarı-Siyah #D97706/#111827`, `Indigo #3B5BDB`, `Kurumsal Lacivert #1E3A8A`, `Koyu Terminal #0D1117`).
